Full-Gym
45–60 min

Legs + Shoulders + Core

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bodyweight squats and reverse lunges
  • → Hip openers, ankle rocks, and arm swings
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first leg movement

Workout

Legs

Barbell Back Squat

Brace and squat under control before driving through mid-foot.

4 sets6–8 reps

Romanian Deadlift

Hinge with a slight knee bend to load the hamstrings.

3 sets10–12 reps

Hack Squat Machine

Use the sled for stable, quad-focused squats.

3 sets8–12 reps

Standing Calf Raise (machine)

Lower into a stretch and rise high onto the toes.

4 sets15–20 reps

Shoulders

Barbell or DB Overhead Press

Press overhead while keeping ribs down and glutes tight.

4 sets8–10 reps

Cable Lateral Raise

Raise one handle sideways to keep tension on the middle delt.

3 sets15 reps

Cable Reverse Fly

Open the arms wide against cable resistance for rear delts.

3 sets12–15 reps

Core

Cable Crunch (kneeling)

Curl the ribcage toward the pelvis instead of pulling with the arms.

3 sets15 reps

Pallof Press (cable)

Press away while resisting the cable rotation.

3 sets12/side reps

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Standing quad and hip-flexor stretch
  • ❄ Hamstring fold with soft knees
  • ❄ Cross-body shoulder stretch
