Back + Biceps

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Cat-cow and thoracic rotations
  • → Scapular rows and controlled hinges
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first row or pull

Workout

Back

Chest-Supported DB Row on Couch

Lie chest-down on a supported couch angle and pull the dumbbells toward your ribs.

4 sets8–12 reps

DB Renegade Row

Hold a strong plank and row one dumbbell at a time without rotating your hips.

3 sets10–12/side reps

DB Row with 2-Second Squeeze

Pause with your elbows pulled back for two seconds to emphasize the mid-back.

3 sets10–12 reps

Prone Y-T-W Raise

Raise your arms into Y, T, and W shapes while lying face-down to train upper-back control.

3 sets12 repsBodyweight

Biceps

DB Zottman Curl

Curl with palms up, rotate at the top, and lower with palms down.

3 sets10–12 reps

Cross-Body Hammer Curl

Curl each dumbbell toward the opposite shoulder with a neutral grip.

3 sets10–12/side reps

DB Drag Curl

Drag the dumbbells up your torso while keeping your elbows behind you.

3 sets10–12 reps

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Child’s pose with side reach
  • ❄ Lat stretch on a support
  • ❄ Gentle neck and biceps stretch

Progressive Overload Log
