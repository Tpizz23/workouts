Chest + Triceps

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bandless arm circles and shoulder openers
  • → 10 slow push-ups with a pause
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the bench or floor press

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest and press it up with a stable upper back.

4 sets6–8 reps

Decline Barbell/DB Press

Press from a slight decline to shift emphasis toward the lower chest.

3 sets8–10 reps

Incline Cable Press

Press the handles upward and inward while keeping tension through the pecs.

3 sets10–12 reps

Machine Chest Press

Drive the handles forward with a controlled stretch and squeeze.

3 sets10–12 reps

Cable Crossover (high to low)

Sweep the cables down and together to finish the lower-chest contraction.

3 sets12–15 reps

Triceps

Close-Grip Bench Press

Use a narrow grip and press with elbows tucked to load the triceps.

3 sets8–10 reps

Single-Arm Cable Pushdown

Lock one elbow at your side and finish each rep strongly.

3 sets12/side reps

Reverse-Grip Cable Pushdown

Use an underhand grip and extend fully for medial-head emphasis.

3 sets12–15 reps

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Doorway pec stretch
  • ❄ Overhead triceps stretch
  • ❄ Slow nasal breathing

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
