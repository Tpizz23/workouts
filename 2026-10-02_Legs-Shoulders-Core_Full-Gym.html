Legs-Shoulders-Core

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 5 minutes easy cycling
  • Two ramp-up sets of Barbell Back Squat
  • Bodyweight lunges and shoulder mobility

Workout

Legs

Barbell Back Squat

Brace hard and squat the bar under control before driving up through both feet.

4 × 6–8Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Romanian Deadlift

Lower the bar along your legs with a small knee bend and a long hamstring stretch.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Leg Press

Lower the sled with control and press through the whole foot without locking out hard.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Standing Calf Raise (machine)

Drop the heels for a stretch, then rise high and pause on the machine.

4 × 15–20Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Shoulders

Barbell or DB Overhead Press

Press the bar or dumbbells overhead while keeping your core tight.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cable Lateral Raise

Raise one handle to shoulder height for constant side-delt tension.

3 × 15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Rear Delt Fly

Support your torso and sweep the dumbbells out to isolate the rear delts.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Core

Cable Crunch (kneeling)

Kneel tall and curl your ribs toward your pelvis against the cable.

3 × 15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Pallof Press (cable)

Press the cable straight out and resist its pull to rotate your torso.

3 × 12/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch
  • Standing quad and hamstring stretch
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
