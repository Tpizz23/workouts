Back + Biceps

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Arm circles and band-free pull-aparts — 60 sec
  • Hip hinges and thoracic rotations — 2 min
  • Light row, 2 easy sets; brace and rehearse the hinge

Workout

Back

Chest-Supported DB Row on Couch

Lie chest-down on a couch angle and pull the dumbbells toward your ribs without swinging.

4 × 8–12Anchor

DB Renegade Row

From a strong plank, row one dumbbell while resisting rotation through your hips.

3 × 8–10

DB Pullover Lat Focus

Lower one dumbbell behind your head and pull it back using your lats.

3 × 10–12

Reverse Snow Angel (prone)

Sweep straight arms from overhead toward your hips while lying face down.

3 × 12Bodyweight

Biceps

DB Concentration Curl

Brace your elbow against your thigh and curl slowly to peak contraction.

3 × 12

DB Reverse Curl

Curl with palms down to train the brachialis and forearms.

3 × 12–15

DB Drag Curl

Pull elbows back as the dumbbells travel up your torso for a strict curl.

3 × 10–12

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Child’s pose with side reach
  • Doorway lat stretch and cross-body shoulder stretch
  • Gentle biceps wall stretch
