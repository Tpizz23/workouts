Back + Biceps

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Cat-cow and thoracic rotations
  • → Scapular rows and controlled hinges
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first row or pull

Workout

Back

Pull-Up or Weighted Pull-Up

Pull from a dead hang until your chin clears the bar without swinging.

4 sets6–10 reps

Barbell Bent-Over Row

Hinge with a flat back and pull the bar toward your lower ribs.

4 sets8–10 reps

Lat Pulldown (wide grip)

Pull the wide bar toward your upper chest while keeping your torso steady.

3 sets10–12 reps

Face Pull (cable, rope)

Pull the rope toward your face and finish with your hands beside your ears.

3 sets12–15 reps

Biceps

Preacher Curl (EZ bar or machine)

Curl from the supported preacher pad and stop short of losing tension.

2 sets12 reps

Cable Hammer Curl (rope)

Curl the rope with a neutral grip while maintaining constant cable tension.

3 sets12–15 reps

EZ-Bar Reverse Curl

Lift with palms down to train the brachialis and forearms.

3 sets12 reps

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Child’s pose with side reach
  • ❄ Lat stretch on a support
  • ❄ Gentle neck and biceps stretch

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
