Legs-Shoulders-Core

Purposeful legs-shoulders-core session. Focus on clean reps, full range of motion, and steady progression.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Bodyweight squats, reverse lunges, hip hinges, shoulder circles, and dead bugs.
  • Use lighter ramp-up sets before the first compound movement.

Workout

Legs

1. DB Goblet Squat

Hold one dumbbell at chest level and squat deeply with your chest tall.

4 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. DB Romanian Deadlift

Hinge at the hips while lowering dumbbells along your legs, then stand by driving your hips forward.

3 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

3. DB Bulgarian Split Squat

Place your rear foot on a chair and lower under control through the front leg.

3 sets8–10/leg

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Shoulders

1. DB Overhead Press

Press dumbbells from shoulder height overhead without leaning back.

4 sets8–10

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. DB Lateral Raise

Raise slightly bent arms out to shoulder height with control.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Core

1. Plank

Brace your abs and hold a straight line from shoulders to heels.

3 sets45–60 secBodyweight

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. Dead Bug

Slowly extend the opposite arm and leg while keeping your lower back down.

3 sets10/sideBodyweight

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Quad stretch, hamstring fold, figure-four glute stretch, and relaxed breathing.
  • Breathe slowly and note any areas needing extra recovery.

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of its range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
