Chest + Triceps

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Arm circles and band-free shoulder openers
  • → 10 slow push-ups
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of your first press

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest and press it up with steady control.

4 × 6–8Log weight:

Incline DB Press

Press dumbbells on a moderate incline to emphasize the upper chest.

3 × 8–10Log weight:

Cable Fly (low to high)

Sweep the handles upward and inward while maintaining a soft elbow bend.

3 × 12–15Log weight:

Pec Deck Machine

Bring the pads together and pause for a hard pec contraction.

3 × 12–15Log weight:

Weighted Dips (chest lean)

Lean forward during weighted dips to emphasize the chest.

3 × 8–12Log weight:

Triceps

Cable Tricep Pushdown (rope)

Push the rope down and separate the ends at lockout.

3 × 12–15Log weight:

EZ-Bar Skull Crusher

Lower the EZ bar toward your forehead with elbows pointed up.

3 × 10–12Log weight:

Overhead Tricep Extension (cable)

Stretch the triceps overhead before extending to straight arms.

3 × 12–15Log weight:

  • ❄ Overhead triceps stretch
