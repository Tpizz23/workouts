Chest + Triceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Cable fly patterning and shoulder circles — 2 min
  • Two progressively heavier bench-press warm-up sets
  • Light pushdowns to prepare elbows

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest and press while keeping shoulder blades packed.

4 × 6–8Anchor

Decline Barbell/DB Press

Press from a slight decline to emphasize lower chest fibers.

3 × 8–10Controlled form

Incline Cable Press

Press cable handles upward and inward for constant-tension upper-chest work.

3 × 10–12Controlled form

Machine Chest Press

Use a stable machine path and pause briefly while squeezing the pecs.

3 × 10–12Controlled form

Cable Crossover (high to low)

Sweep cable handles down and together to contract the lower chest.

3 × 12–15Controlled form

Triceps

Close-Grip Bench Press

Press with a narrow grip and elbows tucked to load the triceps.

3 × 8–10Anchor

Overhead Tricep Extension (cable)

Extend the cable overhead while allowing a controlled elbow bend.

3 × 12–15Controlled form

Reverse-Grip Cable Pushdown

Use an underhand grip and finish each pushdown with a full lockout.

3 × 12–15Controlled form

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway pec stretch and overhead triceps stretch — 30 sec each side
  • Easy shoulder rolls and deep nasal breathing

Progressive Overload Log
