Legs-Shoulders-Core

Train with intent, keep every rep controlled, and build steadily toward 180 lb.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Bodyweight squats, reverse lunges, hip hinges, arm circles, and a 30-second plank.
  • Use the first working set as a gradual ramp-up; stop any painful movement.

Workout

Legs

DB Front Squat

Hold dumbbells at your shoulders and squat deeply while keeping your chest tall.

4 sets8–12 repsWeight:

Quad anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Single-Leg RDL

Balance on one leg and hinge slowly while reaching the dumbbell toward the floor.

3 sets8–10/leg repsWeight:

Unilateral hinge · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Hip Thrust on Couch

Rest your upper back on the couch and drive your hips upward to full extension.

3 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Glute volume · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Single-Leg Calf Raise

Rise onto one forefoot while holding a dumbbell and pause at the top.

3 sets15–20/side repsWeight:

Calf work · Rest 60–90 sec.

Shoulders

DB Push Press

Use a small knee dip to help drive the dumbbells overhead, then control the descent.

3 sets8–10 repsWeight:

Power press · Rest 60–90 sec.

Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise

Hold support and lean away slightly to lengthen the side-delt movement.

3 sets10–12/side repsWeight:

Side delts · Rest 60–90 sec.

Prone Rear Delt Y-Raise

Lie face down and raise your arms into a Y to strengthen the rear shoulders.

3 sets12 repsWeight:

Rear delts · Rest 60–90 sec.

Core

DB Suitcase Carry/March

Hold one heavy dumbbell and march slowly while resisting side bending.

3 sets30 sec/side repsWeight:

Anti-lateral flexion · Rest 60–90 sec.

Plank Shoulder Tap

From a plank, tap opposite shoulders while keeping your hips as still as possible.

3 sets20 taps repsBodyweight

Anti-rotation · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Quad stretch, hamstring fold, figure-four glute stretch, and gentle shoulder reach.
  • Hold each comfortable stretch for 20–30 seconds and breathe slowly.

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 5 lb next time.
