Chest-Triceps

Train with intent, keep every rep controlled, and build steadily toward 180 lb.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Band-free arm circles, shoulder rolls, 2 light press-up sets, and 8 slow incline push-ups.
  • Use the first working set as a gradual ramp-up; stop any painful movement.

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Press the bar from mid-chest to locked-out arms while keeping your shoulder blades set.

4 sets6–8 repsWeight:

Heavy anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

Pec Deck Machine

Bring the machine pads together with a controlled squeeze across the chest.

3 sets8–10 repsWeight:

Upper-chest strength · Rest 60–90 sec.

Weighted Dips (chest lean)

Lean slightly forward as you lower and press between parallel bars to load the pecs.

3 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Lower-chest tension · Rest 60–90 sec.

Incline Cable Press

Press the cable handles upward and inward from a low start to keep upper-chest tension.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Controlled volume · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cable Fly (low to high)

Sweep the cable handles upward and together in front of your chest.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Chest bonus · Rest 60–90 sec.

Triceps

Assisted Dip Machine

Use the machine assistance to lower and press through a deep, controlled dip.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Long-head stretch · Rest 60–90 sec.

Reverse-Grip Cable Pushdown

Push the underhand cable attachment down while keeping your elbows pinned.

3 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Lengthened work · Rest 60–90 sec.

Single-Arm Cable Pushdown

Keep the upper arm still and finish each single-arm pushdown with a hard lockout.

3 sets12/side repsWeight:

Unilateral finish · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway pec stretch, overhead triceps stretch, and slow nasal breathing.
  • Hold each comfortable stretch for 20–30 seconds and breathe slowly.

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 5 lb next time.
