Legs-Shoulders-Core

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 3 minutes bodyweight squats and marching
  • Two light sets of DB Goblet Squat
  • Hip hinges, reverse lunges, and shoulder circles

Workout

Legs

DB Goblet Squat

Hold one dumbbell at your chest and squat deeply with your chest tall.

4 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Romanian Deadlift

Hinge back with a flat back until your hamstrings stretch, then stand tall.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Bulgarian Split Squat

With your rear foot elevated, lower under control and drive through the front foot.

3 × 8–10/legWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Single-Leg Calf Raise

Rise onto one forefoot while holding a dumbbell and pause at the top.

3 × 15–20/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Shoulders

DB Overhead Press

Press the dumbbells overhead while keeping ribs down and wrists stacked.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Lateral Raise

Raise light dumbbells out to shoulder height with a soft elbow bend.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Rear Delt Fly

Hinge forward and open the arms wide to contract the rear shoulders.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Core

Dead Bug

Extend opposite arm and leg while keeping your lower back gently braced.

3 × 10/sideBodyweight

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Suitcase Carry/March

Hold one heavy dumbbell and march without leaning away from the load.

3 × 30 sec/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch
  • Standing quad and hamstring stretch
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
