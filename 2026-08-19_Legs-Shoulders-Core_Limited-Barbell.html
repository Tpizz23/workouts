Limited-Barbell
45–60 min

Legs + Shoulders + Core

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bodyweight squats and reverse lunges
  • → Hip openers, ankle rocks, and arm swings
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first leg movement

Workout

Legs

DB Bulgarian Split Squat

Lower under control with your rear foot elevated and drive through the front heel.

3 sets8–10/leg reps

DB Sumo Deadlift

Use a wide stance and hinge the dumbbells up while squeezing the glutes.

3 sets10–12 reps

DB Step-Up

Step onto a sturdy chair or bench substitute and stand tall without pushing off the rear leg.

3 sets10/leg reps

Wall Sit with DB

Hold a dumbbell while keeping your back against the wall and thighs parallel.

3 sets30–45 sec reps

Shoulders

DB Push Press

Use a small knee dip to help drive the dumbbells overhead.

3 sets8–10 reps

Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise

Lean from a support and raise one dumbbell through a long side-delt arc.

3 sets10–12/side reps

Prone Rear Delt Y-Raise

Lie face-down and lift light arms into a controlled Y shape.

3 sets12 reps

Core

DB Suitcase Carry/March

Hold one heavy dumbbell and march slowly while resisting side bending.

3 sets30 sec/side reps

Bird-Dog

Reach opposite arm and leg while keeping your hips square and spine steady.

3 sets10/side repsBodyweight

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Hip-flexor and quad stretch
  • ❄ Seated hamstring stretch
  • ❄ Calf stretch and relaxed breathing

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
