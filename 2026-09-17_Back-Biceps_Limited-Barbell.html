Back-Biceps

Purposeful back-biceps session. Focus on clean reps, full range of motion, and steady progression.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Cat-cow, thoracic rotations, hip hinges, and two light row/pulldown sets.
  • Use lighter ramp-up sets before the first compound movement.

Workout

Back

1. DB Bent-Over Row

Hinge with a flat back and pull both dumbbells toward your ribs.

4 sets8–10

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. Single-Arm DB Row

Brace one hand on a chair and row the dumbbell toward your hip.

3 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

3. DB Romanian Deadlift

Hinge with dumbbells to train the posterior chain and reinforce back tension.

3 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

4. Reverse Snow Angel (prone)

Lie face-down and sweep your arms in an arc to engage the upper back.

3 sets12Bodyweight

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Biceps

1. DB Curl

Curl with palms forward and rotate fully into a hard squeeze at the top.

3 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. DB Hammer Curl

Curl with neutral palms to emphasize the brachialis and forearms.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

3. DB Concentration Curl

Brace your elbow against your inner thigh and curl slowly through the full range.

3 sets12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Child’s pose with side reach, lat stretch, and relaxed biceps extension.
  • Breathe slowly and note any areas needing extra recovery.

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of its range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
