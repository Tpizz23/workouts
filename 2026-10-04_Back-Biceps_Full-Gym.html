Back-Biceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 5 minutes easy incline walk
  • Two light sets of Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown
  • Cable face pulls and controlled shoulder circles

Workout

Back

T-Bar Row

Pull the supported or landmine bar toward your chest while keeping your spine braced.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown

Pull the neutral handles to your upper chest while keeping your shoulders down.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Meadows Row

Use a landmine one-arm row to pull through a long, angled range of motion.

3 × 10/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Face Pull (cable, rope)

Pull the rope toward your face and rotate your hands outward to train rear delts.

3 × 15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Biceps

Preacher Curl (EZ bar or machine)

Rest your upper arms on the pad and curl without letting the elbows lift.

2 × 12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cable Curl (straight bar)

Curl the straight bar through constant cable tension with strict posture.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

EZ-Bar Reverse Curl

Curl with palms down to emphasize the brachialis and forearms.

3 × 12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Child’s pose with side reach
  • Cross-body rear-delt stretch
  • Gentle biceps wall stretch

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
