Chest-Triceps

Purposeful chest-triceps session. Focus on clean reps, full range of motion, and steady progression.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Arm circles, bandless pull-aparts, scapular push-ups, and two light press-up sets.
  • Use lighter ramp-up sets before the first compound movement.

Workout

Chest

1. Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest and press it upward while keeping your shoulder blades set.

4 sets6–8

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. Incline DB Press

Press dumbbells on a 30–45° incline to emphasize the upper chest.

3 sets8–10

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

3. Cable Fly (low to high)

Sweep the handles upward and inward, squeezing the upper chest.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

4. Pec Deck Machine

Bring the machine pads together with a controlled chest contraction.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Triceps

1. Cable Tricep Pushdown (rope)

Drive the rope down and separate the ends while keeping your elbows near your sides.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

2. EZ-Bar Skull Crusher

Lower the EZ-bar toward your forehead with fixed elbows, then extend smoothly.

3 sets10–12

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

3. Overhead Tricep Extension (cable)

Face away from the cable and extend overhead through a full triceps stretch.

3 sets12–15

Move with control; stop 1–2 reps before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway chest stretch, overhead triceps stretch, and gentle shoulder rolls.
  • Breathe slowly and note any areas needing extra recovery.

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of its range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
