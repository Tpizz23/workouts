#f5a623, Wednesday
Full-Gym
45–60 min

Legs + Shoulders + Core

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bodyweight squats and reverse lunges
  • → Hip openers, ankle rocks, and arm swings
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first leg movement

Workout

Legs

Hack Squat Machine

Lower into the hack squat with your knees tracking over your toes.

3 sets8–12 reps

Romanian Deadlift

Hinge with a flat back and lower the bar until your hamstrings stretch.

3 sets10–12 reps

Bulgarian Split Squat

Lower the rear-knee toward the floor while driving through the front foot.

3 sets8–10/leg reps

Seated Calf Raise

Pause at the top and stretch fully at the bottom with bent knees.

4 sets12–15 reps

Shoulders

Machine Shoulder Press

Press the handles overhead from a stable seat without shrugging.

3 sets10–12 reps

Machine Lateral Raise

Raise the pads out to shoulder height for stable side-delt tension.

3 sets12–15 reps

Face Pull to External Rotation

Pull the rope toward your face and rotate your hands back to train rear delts.

3 sets12–15 reps

Core

Ab Wheel Rollout

Roll forward only as far as you can keep your ribs and hips braced.

3 sets8–12 reps

Pallof Press (cable)

Press the cable straight out while resisting the pull into rotation.

3 sets12/side reps

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Hip-flexor and quad stretch
  • ❄ Seated hamstring stretch
  • ❄ Calf stretch and relaxed breathing

Progressive Overload Log

When every set reaches the top of the rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
