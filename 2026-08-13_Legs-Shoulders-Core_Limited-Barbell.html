Legs + Shoulders + Core

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bodyweight squats and reverse lunges
  • → Hip openers, ankle rocks, and arm swings
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the first leg movement

Workout

Legs

DB Front Squat

Hold dumbbells at the shoulders and squat deeply with a tall chest.

4 sets8–12 reps

DB Single-Leg RDL

Balance on one leg and hinge slowly to load glute and hamstring.

3 sets8–10/leg reps

DB Step-Up

Step onto a sturdy platform and drive through the working foot.

3 sets10/leg reps

DB Single-Leg Calf Raise

Rise onto one forefoot and pause at the top.

3 sets15–20 reps

Shoulders

DB Push Press

Use a small knee dip to drive dumbbells overhead.

3 sets8–10 reps

Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise

Lean from support and raise one dumbbell through the side-delt arc.

3 sets10–12/side reps

Core

DB Suitcase Carry/March

March while holding one heavy dumbbell and resisting side-bending.

3 sets30 sec/side reps

Bird-Dog

Reach opposite arm and leg slowly with square hips.

3 sets10/side repsBodyweight

  • ❄ Hamstring fold with soft knees
  • ❄ Cross-body shoulder stretch
