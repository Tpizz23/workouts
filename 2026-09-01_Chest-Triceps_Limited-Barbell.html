Chest + Triceps

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Arm circles, scapular push-ups, and doorway pec openers
  • Two light press sets with a pause at the bottom
  • Elbow and shoulder activation — 60 sec

Workout

Chest

DB Floor Press

Press dumbbells from the floor while keeping elbows controlled and shoulder blades packed.

4 × 8–10Anchor

Incline Push-Up (feet elevated)

Elevate your feet and lower your chest as one unit to bias the upper pecs.

3 × 10–15Bodyweight

DB Pullover (floor)

Lower a dumbbell overhead and pull it back over your chest to stretch chest and serratus.

3 × 12

Deficit Push-Up on DB Handles

Use dumbbell handles to increase depth while maintaining a rigid push-up position.

3 × 8–12Bodyweight

Triceps

DB Skull Crusher (floor)

Bend only at the elbows to lower dumbbells beside your head, then extend.

3 × 10–12

DB Overhead Tricep Extension

Lower one or two dumbbells behind your head and extend to full lockout.

3 × 12–15

DB Kickback

Hinge forward and straighten the elbows behind you for a hard top contraction.

3 × 12–15

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Doorway chest stretch at two arm angles
  • Overhead triceps stretch
  • Slow nasal breathing with shoulders relaxed

Progressive Overload Log

Add 5 lb to the floor press when all four sets reach 10 reps cleanly.
