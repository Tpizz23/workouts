Back

Hinge at the hips and pull both dumbbells toward your ribs.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

3 × 10–12/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Biceps

DB Curl

Curl the dumbbells while turning your palms up and squeezing at the top.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Hammer Curl

Curl with neutral palms to build the biceps and brachialis.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Concentration Curl

Brace your elbow against your inner thigh and curl one dumbbell with control.

3 × 12/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.
