Back-Biceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 5 minutes easy rower
  • Two ramp-up sets of Barbell Bent-Over Row
  • Band-free scapular pulls and rear-delt activation

Workout

Back

Pull-Up or Weighted Pull-Up

Pull from a dead hang until your chin clears the bar without swinging.

4 × 6–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Barbell Bent-Over Row

Brace your torso and pull the bar toward your lower ribs.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Seated Cable Row (V-bar)

Drive the V-handle toward your waist while keeping your chest tall.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Straight-Arm Pulldown

Keep your elbows softly bent and sweep the cable down toward your thighs.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Biceps

Barbell Curl

Stand tall and curl the bar without rocking your torso.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Incline DB Curl

Let the arms hang behind you on an incline bench, then curl with control.

3 × 12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cable Hammer Curl (rope)

Curl the rope with neutral palms and constant cable tension.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Child’s pose with side reach
  • Cross-body rear-delt stretch
  • Gentle biceps wall stretch

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
