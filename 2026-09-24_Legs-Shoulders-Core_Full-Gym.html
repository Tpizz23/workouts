Legs-Shoulders-Core

Train with intent, keep every rep controlled, and build steadily toward 180 lb.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Bodyweight squats, reverse lunges, hip hinges, arm circles, and a 30-second plank.
  • Use the first working set as a gradual ramp-up; stop any painful movement.

Workout

Legs

Barbell Back Squat

Brace hard and squat the bar under control before driving through the floor.

4 sets6–8 repsWeight:

Heavy anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

Romanian Deadlift

Hinge the bar down with a slight knee bend until the hamstrings are fully loaded.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Hamstrings/glutes · Rest 60–90 sec.

Hack Squat Machine

Lower under control on the hack-squat sled and drive evenly through both feet.

3 sets8–12 repsWeight:

Quad volume · Rest 60–90 sec.

Seated Calf Raise

Drop the heels for a stretch, then rise and pause through the calves.

4 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Calf work · Rest 60–90 sec.

Shoulders

Barbell or DB Overhead Press

Press from shoulder height while keeping your ribs stacked over your pelvis.

4 sets8–10 repsWeight:

Shoulder anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cable Lateral Raise

Raise one handle out to shoulder height while keeping constant cable tension.

3 sets15 repsWeight:

Side delts · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cable Reverse Fly

Open the cables wide with soft elbows to contract the rear delts.

3 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Rear delts · Rest 60–90 sec.

Core

Ab Wheel Rollout

Roll forward only as far as you can keep your ribs tucked and spine neutral.

3 sets8–12 repsWeight:

Anti-extension · Rest 60–90 sec.

Pallof Press (cable)

Press the cable straight out and resist the rotation pulling you sideways.

3 sets12/side repsWeight:

Anti-rotation · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Quad stretch, hamstring fold, figure-four glute stretch, and gentle shoulder reach.
  • Hold each comfortable stretch for 20–30 seconds and breathe slowly.

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 5 lb next time.
