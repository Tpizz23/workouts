Chest-Triceps

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 3 minutes jumping jacks or marching with arm swings
  • Two light sets of DB Floor Press
  • Push-up plus and shoulder circles

Workout

Chest

DB Floor Press

Press dumbbells from the floor while keeping your elbows controlled against the ground.

4 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Wide Push-Up

Lower your chest between wide-set hands and press back up as one unit.

3 × 12–15Bodyweight

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Floor Fly

Open the dumbbells in a shallow arc for a controlled chest stretch, then squeeze them together.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Incline Push-Up (feet elevated)

Elevate your feet and perform push-ups to shift more work to the upper chest.

3 × 10–15Bodyweight

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Squeeze Press

Press two dumbbells together throughout the movement to keep constant pec tension.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Triceps

DB Skull Crusher (floor)

Lower the dumbbells beside your head and extend your elbows without flaring them.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Overhead Tricep Extension

Lower one or two dumbbells behind your head and extend to lockout.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Diamond Push-Up

Use a close diamond hand position and keep elbows near your ribs as you press.

3 × 8–15Bodyweight

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway pec stretch
  • Overhead triceps stretch
  • Easy breathing for 2 minutes

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
