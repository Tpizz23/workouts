Chest + Triceps

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Scapular push-ups and arm swings — 90 sec
  • Light single-arm presses, 1 set per side
  • Slow push-up rehearsal and elbow circles

Workout

Chest

Single-Arm DB Floor Press

Press one dumbbell while resisting torso rotation and keeping the opposite shoulder grounded.

4 × 8–10/sideAnchor

Decline Push-Up (hands elevated on couch)

Place hands on a couch and lower your chest to bias the lower pecs.

3 × 10–15Bodyweight

DB Squeeze Press

Press the dumbbells together throughout each rep to keep constant pec tension.

3 × 10–12

DB Floor Fly with Pause

Open the arms under control, pause near the floor, then squeeze the dumbbells together.

3 × 10–12

Triceps

Close-Grip Push-Up

Keep elbows close as you lower and press to load the triceps.

3 × 10–15Bodyweight

DB Tate Press

Lower dumbbells toward the chest with elbows flared, then squeeze them back up.

3 × 10–12

DB Rolling Tricep Extension

Roll dumbbells beside your head to create a stretch before extending the elbows.

3 × 10–12

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Low doorway pec stretch
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch
  • Overhead triceps stretch and slow breathing

Progressive Overload Log

Use the same load until all single-arm sets reach 10 controlled reps.
