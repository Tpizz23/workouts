Chest + Triceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Light cardio and shoulder mobility — 2 min
  • Empty-bar press and two gradual ramp-up sets
  • Light cable fly to groove the squeeze

Workout

Chest

Incline DB Press

Press dumbbells from a 30–45° bench while keeping shoulder blades pinned.

4 × 8–10Anchor

Decline Barbell/DB Press

Press on a decline bench to emphasize the lower portion of the chest.

3 × 8–10

Pec Deck Machine

Bring the pads together with a controlled stretch and hard peak squeeze.

3 × 12–15

DB Squeeze Press

Press dumbbells together as you press up to keep the pecs working continuously.

3 × 10–12

Triceps

EZ-Bar Skull Crusher

Lower the EZ bar toward your forehead with elbows pointed up, then extend.

3 × 10–12

Overhead Tricep Extension (cable)

Face away from the cable and extend overhead through a deep triceps stretch.

3 × 12–15

Assisted Dip Machine

Lower and press through a supported dip path while keeping elbows close.

3 × 8–12

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Doorway chest stretch
  • Overhead triceps stretch
  • Light pec-deck reverse stretch

Progressive Overload Log

When all incline-press sets reach 10 reps, add 5 lb per dumbbell if form stays strong.
