Back + Biceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Scapular pull-ups and arm circles — 90 sec
  • Light cable rows and pulldowns — 2 easy sets each
  • Thoracic rotations; brace before heavy pulls

Workout

Back

T-Bar Row

Brace your torso and drive elbows back to row the bar toward your lower chest.

4 × 8–10Anchor

Neutral-Grip Lat Pulldown

Pull the neutral handles toward your upper chest while keeping your ribs controlled.

3 × 10–12

Meadows Row

Use a staggered stance to pull one end of a landmine bar toward your hip.

3 × 10/side

Face Pull (cable, rope)

Pull the rope toward your face and rotate thumbs behind you to train rear delts.

3 × 15

Biceps

Preacher Curl (EZ bar or machine)

Curl from the supported preacher pad without letting your shoulders help.

3 × 10–12

Cable Hammer Curl (rope)

Curl a rope with neutral hands and keep constant tension through the set.

3 × 12–15

EZ-Bar Reverse Curl

Lift the EZ bar with palms down to finish the forearms and brachialis.

3 × 12

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Passive hang or supported lat stretch
  • Cross-body rear-delt stretch
  • Gentle biceps and forearm stretch

Progressive Overload Log

Reach the top of the rep range on all sets before adding 5 lb.
