Chest + Triceps

Train with intent, record your work, and build quality muscle for the long term.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • → Bandless arm circles and shoulder openers
  • → 10 slow push-ups with a pause
  • → 2 light ramp-up sets of the bench or floor press

Workout

Chest

DB Floor Press

Press heavy dumbbells from the floor while keeping your elbows controlled.

4 sets8–10 reps

Incline Push-Up (feet elevated)

Elevate your feet and lower your chest slowly to emphasize the upper pecs.

3 sets10–15 repsBodyweight

DB Squeeze Press

Press the dumbbells together throughout each rep to keep constant pec tension.

3 sets10–12 reps

DB Pullover (floor)

Lower one dumbbell behind your head and pull it back over your chest.

3 sets12 reps

DB Floor Fly with Pause

Open the arms with control, pause near the floor, then squeeze the chest closed.

3 sets10–12 reps

Triceps

DB Rolling Tricep Extension

Roll the dumbbells slightly behind your head before extending your elbows.

3 sets10–12 reps

Single-Arm Overhead DB Extension

Lower one dumbbell behind your head and extend without flaring the elbow.

3 sets10–12/side reps

Diamond Push-Up

Use a close hand position and keep your elbows tight to bias the triceps.

3 sets8–15 repsBodyweight

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • ❄ Doorway pec stretch
  • ❄ Overhead triceps stretch
  • ❄ Slow nasal breathing
