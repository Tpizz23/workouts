Legs + Shoulders + Core

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Bike or treadmill — 2 min
  • Bodyweight squats, hip hinges, and shoulder circles
  • Two ramp-up sets for the first squat

Workout

Legs

Hack Squat Machine

Lower under control and drive through the mid-foot for a quad-focused squat.

4 × 8–12Anchor

Romanian Deadlift

Push hips back with a slight knee bend until the hamstrings stretch, then stand tall.

3 × 10–12

Bulgarian Split Squat

With your rear foot elevated, lower straight down and press through the front leg.

3 × 8–10/leg

Seated Calf Raise

Move through a deep ankle stretch and squeeze the calves at lockout.

4 × 12–15

Shoulders

Machine Shoulder Press

Press the handles overhead without shrugging, keeping the motion smooth.

3 × 10–12

Cable Lateral Raise

Raise one arm out to the side against steady cable tension.

3 × 15/side

Core

Pallof Press (cable)

Press the cable away from your chest while resisting rotation.

3 × 12/side

Ab Wheel Rollout

Roll forward only as far as you can keep your ribs and hips braced.

3 × 8–12

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch
  • Seated hamstring and standing quad stretch
  • Overhead triceps/lat stretch with slow breathing

Progressive Overload Log

When all hack-squat sets reach 12 reps, add 5–10 lb next session.
