Chest + Triceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Light cardio and shoulder circles — 2 min
  • Empty-bar bench press, 2 ramp-up sets
  • Band-free scapular push-ups and cable warm-up

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest with a stable upper back and press smoothly.

4 × 6–8Anchor

Incline Cable Press

Press cables upward and inward on an incline path for constant upper-chest tension.

3 × 10–12

Cable Crossover (high to low)

Bring high cable handles down and together while squeezing the lower chest.

3 × 12–15

Machine Chest Press

Use the stable machine to press through a controlled range and finish with a pec squeeze.

3 × 10–12

Triceps

Close-Grip Bench Press

Use a narrower grip and tucked elbows to shift pressing demand toward triceps.

3 × 8–10

Single-Arm Cable Pushdown

Press one cable handle to lockout while keeping the upper arm still.

3 × 12/side

Reverse-Grip Cable Pushdown

Use an underhand grip and extend fully to emphasize the medial triceps head.

3 × 12–15

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Doorway chest stretch
  • Overhead triceps stretch
  • Gentle shoulder external-rotation stretch
