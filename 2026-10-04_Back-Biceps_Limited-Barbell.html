Back-Biceps

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 3 minutes marching with reach and trunk rotation
  • One light set of Single-Arm DB Row per side
  • Band-free scapular retractions and shoulder circles

Workout

Back

Chest-Supported DB Row on Couch

Lie chest-down on a couch incline and pull the dumbbells toward your sides.

4 × 8–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Row with 2-Second Squeeze

Pause with your elbows back for two seconds on every dumbbell row.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Renegade Row

From a plank on the dumbbells, row one weight while resisting torso rotation.

3 × 8–10/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Prone Y-T-W Raise

Lie face down and form slow Y, T, and W arm shapes to control the upper back.

3 × 8 each shapeBodyweight

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Biceps

DB Zottman Curl

Curl palms-up, then rotate palms down for the controlled lowering phase.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

DB Drag Curl

Drag the dumbbells up your torso with elbows traveling behind you.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cross-Body Hammer Curl

Curl each dumbbell toward the opposite shoulder for a strong neutral-grip finish.

3 × 10–12/sideWeight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Child’s pose with side reach
  • Cross-body rear-delt stretch
  • Gentle biceps wall stretch

Progressive Overload Log
