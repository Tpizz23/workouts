Legs + Shoulders + Core

Limited-Barbell45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Bodyweight squats and reverse lunges — 90 sec
  • Hip hinges, ankle rocks, and arm circles — 2 min
  • Two light goblet-squat practice sets

Workout

Legs

DB Goblet Squat

Hold one dumbbell at your chest and squat deep while keeping your torso tall.

4 × 10–12Anchor

DB Single-Leg RDL

Hinge on one leg with a flat back to load the glute and hamstring.

3 × 8–10/leg

DB Step-Up

Step onto a sturdy platform and drive through the working heel under control.

3 × 10/leg

DB Single-Leg Calf Raise

Rise onto one forefoot through a full stretch and pause at the top.

3 × 15–20

Shoulders

DB Push Press

Use a small knee dip to help drive dumbbells overhead, then control them down.

3 × 8–10

Lean-Away DB Lateral Raise

Lean from a support and raise one dumbbell out to extend side-delt tension.

3 × 10–12/side

Core

Dead Bug

Extend opposite arm and leg while keeping your lower back gently braced.

3 × 10/sideBodyweight

DB Suitcase Carry/March

Hold one heavy dumbbell and march without leaning toward the weight.

3 × 30 sec/side

Cool-Down · 2 min

  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch
  • Standing quad and hamstring stretch
  • Cross-body shoulder stretch and slow breathing

Progressive Overload Log

Add 5 lb only when every squat set reaches 12 clean reps.
