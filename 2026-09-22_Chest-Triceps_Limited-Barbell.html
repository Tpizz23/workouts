Chest-Triceps

Train with intent, keep every rep controlled, and build steadily toward 180 lb.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Band-free arm circles, shoulder rolls, 2 light press-up sets, and 8 slow incline push-ups.
  • Use the first working set as a gradual ramp-up; stop any painful movement.

Workout

Chest

DB Floor Press

Primary press: drive both dumbbells upward from the floor with controlled elbows.

4 sets8–10 repsWeight:

Heavy anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

Incline Push-Up (feet elevated)

Place your feet on the couch and lower your chest under control.

3 sets10–15 repsBodyweight

Upper-chest emphasis · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Floor Fly with Pause

Open your arms wide on the floor, pause briefly, then squeeze the dumbbells together.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Chest stretch · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Pullover (floor)

Lower one dumbbell behind your head and pull it back over your chest using your lats and pecs.

3 sets12 repsWeight:

Chest/serratus · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Squeeze Press

Press the dumbbells together throughout each rep to keep constant tension on the pecs.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Chest bonus · Rest 60–90 sec.

Triceps

DB Skull Crusher (floor)

Bend only at the elbows to lower the dumbbells beside your head, then extend.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Controlled stretch · Rest 60–90 sec.

Single-Arm Overhead DB Extension

Lower one dumbbell behind your head and extend fully without arching your back.

3 sets10–12/side repsWeight:

Balanced isolation · Rest 60–90 sec.

Diamond Push-Up

Use a close diamond hand position and keep your elbows tucked as you press.

3 sets8–15 repsBodyweight

Bodyweight finisher · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway pec stretch, overhead triceps stretch, and slow nasal breathing.
  • Hold each comfortable stretch for 20–30 seconds and breathe slowly.

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 5 lb next time.
