Chest-Triceps

Full-Gym45–60 minBulk: 165 → 180 lb

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • 5 minutes light rowing
  • Two ramp-up sets of Barbell Bench Press
  • Cable fly-throughs and shoulder circles

Workout

Chest

Barbell Bench Press

Lower the bar to mid-chest with control and press it up over your shoulders.

4 × 6–8Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Incline DB Press

Press dumbbells on a 30–45° incline to emphasize the upper chest.

3 × 8–10Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cable Fly (low to high)

Sweep the handles upward and together while maintaining a full chest stretch.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Pec Deck Machine

Bring the pads together and squeeze your chest at the peak.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Weighted Dips (chest lean)

Lean slightly forward as you lower and press to bias the chest on dips.

3 × 8–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Triceps

Cable Tricep Pushdown (rope)

Press the rope down and separate the ends at lockout for a full triceps squeeze.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

EZ-Bar Skull Crusher

Lower the EZ bar toward your forehead with elbows steady, then extend.

3 × 10–12Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Overhead Tricep Extension (cable)

Face away from the cable and extend overhead through a deep triceps stretch.

3 × 12–15Weight:

Rest 60–90 sec; use a controlled tempo and stop one rep before form breaks.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Doorway pec stretch
  • Overhead triceps stretch
  • Easy breathing for 2 minutes

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of its rep range with good form, add 5 lb next time.
