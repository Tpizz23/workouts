Back-Biceps

Train with intent, keep every rep controlled, and build steadily toward 180 lb.

Warm-Up · 5 min

  • Cat-cow, thoracic rotations, scapular push-ups, hip hinges, and 2 light row sets.
  • Use the first working set as a gradual ramp-up; stop any painful movement.

Workout

Back

DB Bent-Over Row

Hinge with a flat back and pull both dumbbells toward your lower ribs.

4 sets8–10 repsWeight:

Heavy anchor · Rest 60–90 sec.

Single-Arm DB Row

Brace one hand on the couch and row the dumbbell toward your hip.

3 sets10–12/side repsWeight:

Lat strength · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Pullover Lat Focus

Lower the dumbbell behind your head and pull it back using your lats.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Lat isolation · Rest 60–90 sec.

Prone Y-T-W Raise

Lie face down and slowly form Y, T, and W shapes with your arms.

3 sets8 each shape repsBodyweight

Upper-back control · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Row with 2-Second Squeeze

Row both dumbbells and hold your elbows back for two seconds on every rep.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Mid-back squeeze · Rest 60–90 sec.

Biceps

DB Curl

Curl with palms forward and rotate fully into a strong squeeze at the top.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Strict curl · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Hammer Curl

Keep a neutral grip while curling to train the biceps and brachialis.

3 sets12–15 repsWeight:

Neutral grip · Rest 60–90 sec.

DB Zottman Curl

Curl palms-up and lower slowly with palms-down to challenge the forearms.

3 sets10–12 repsWeight:

Finisher · Rest 60–90 sec.

Cool-Down / Stretch · 2 min

  • Child’s pose lat reach, kneeling lat stretch, biceps wall stretch, and easy breathing.
  • Hold each comfortable stretch for 20–30 seconds and breathe slowly.

Progressive Overload Log

If every set reaches the top of the range with clean form, add 5 lb next time.
